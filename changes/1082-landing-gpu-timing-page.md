The landing concept lab has a GPU timing page, `bench.html` beside the lab. Opened on a device, it times each scene at its defaults on that device: how long its shader program takes to compile and link, then how long its frames take on the GPU (or between frames, where the browser offers no GPU timer), next to the graphics renderer and its limits, in a report to copy or download. It sends nothing anywhere.
