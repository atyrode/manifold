The landing concept's lab edits the engine's documents. While the canvas is on screen, the Controls show the document instead of a scene's knobs: its name with saving it in this browser, a new one from the canvas's own, export to a `.json` file and import from one (a file naming a kind this build lacks, or more of a kind than it allows, is refused with the reason); the objects it holds, under the camera and the World, to select, duplicate or delete; Add, every kind by family with a search, a kind the document cannot take shown disabled with why (one ground, a kind's most); the selected object's place, turn and size as fields whose names scrub, and its kind's options alone as knobs; the camera's place, turn, tilt and field of view with "look at selection"; then the World's groups and the Look as ever. In the picture a click selects (corner brackets mark the object's bounds), a drag moves the selected object across whatever lies under the pointer (Shift turns it, Alt or Shift with the wheel sizes it), a drag elsewhere orbits about the point under the pointer, a right or middle drag pans, the wheel dollies, W A S D Q E fly and Esc lets go. Undo steps through the document and the look as one history, a drag, a wheel run or a flight one step. Documents saved in this browser show on the Scenes view's new Canvas shelf, each drawn as a plan from above.
