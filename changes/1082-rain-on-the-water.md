In the landing concept lab, the World's rain and wind now reach the scenes seen from above: the pond, the ripple tank and the tide pool list the World's own `rain` and `wind` knobs, and every raindrop strikes their water as a real impulse, so its ring spreads, crosses the other rings and fades. The pond's analytic water gains rain rings on a fixed beat per drop site, carried by the current and summed with its own drop rings; the ripple tank's wave sim takes small dips at hashed places on its fixed step, as many as the rain asks, and the wind stirs its surface in gusts; the tide pool's rain, which before only drew rings over its shallow-water sea, is now the World's and splashes into the simulated sea itself, while the wind roughens its ripples. Rain compiles in only while it falls, and every scene's default picture is unchanged.
