The landing concept's Skyward is now a preset of the `outdoor` composer (ADR 0062 Phase 2c), its rig lying on the road and pitched up toward the zenith, in three layers locked to it: the `crossroads` backdrop (the facades, the World's sky over the strip between them under the city's glow, the airliners beyond the deck, the road under you in the passage, the street's birds and the banner with its M, the street's air and the World's fog laid along its own rays), the `streetTree` and the `tramway` (wires, trams, lamps and the signal gantry). It draws the same pixels as before at its defaults, in `view=source`, at night, with an eye planet, with the banner's M by day and night and with its city and seed knobs under their new keys; in rain 1.4 % of cells differ, where the World's drops now stop at the tree, the wires and the gantry and its nearest drops are dimmed as the World dims them, and the World's snow and fog take their own colours and fall to the composed depth (D10). The composer gained `precip.seen`, the axes a backdrop's camera sees the World's rain and snow through, drawn by the World's new `worldPrecipAxes` (`worldPrecipSeen` its case of one set of axes, unthinned; the dunes' overhead view draws as before); the World's level flights are left out of it, its birds knobs flying the street's own pigeons and swifts seen from below, and it leaves `worldGates` for the composer's per-setting World gates. Keys: `windows`, `traffic` and `greenery` became `crossroadsWindows`, `crossroadsTraffic` and `crossroadsGreenery`, `mark` `crossroadsMark`, `citySeed` `seed`; `towers`, `planes` and `snowOverhead` stay.
