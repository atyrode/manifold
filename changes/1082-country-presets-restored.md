Six of the landing concept lab's Countryside presets lost settings when the countryside's controls changed, and now look as they were meant to. Flower valley, lavender rows and sunflowers give all the fields to their flower again, with the woods at their usual third of the land, instead of leaving most of the valley to woodland; mixed patchwork shares the fields among crops, wildflowers, lavender and sunflowers in its saved proportions again; woodland valley is wooded over half its land, with its fields split between crops and pasture and a buzzard passing often; and riverside has its broad river, its crops and cattle pasture and its birds back. Every baseline preset now also lights up in the Presets view once it is loaded.
