The landing concept's slime mould now grows over a live adaptive flow network, the model of Tero et al.'s Tokyo rail experiment that the scene cites: its tubes swell with the flow they carry between the oat flakes and wither without it. The opening picture is this network, joining the flakes with trunks, Steiner junctions and a few loops, and it stays on under the specks as the faint film they ride, so the veins now follow the food: about a minute after a flake starts to be eaten, the veins that served only it have faded, and a new flake is joined within a few seconds, where before the opening network's veins to eaten flakes stayed for good. The evaporation knob also sets how fast unused veins wither. Laying out a fresh dish (a new layout or seed, a big resize) takes about a third of the main-thread time it did.
