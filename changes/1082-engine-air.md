The landing concept's engine has its air: wind at any point of the world (still at the ground, stronger with height, gusting with the World's gust field), height fog thinning upward and glowing toward the sun, rain and snow as particles falling and blowing through the world around the eye (near drops pass far ones as the camera moves, and nothing behind a surface shows), heavy rain veiling the distance, and wet, puddled and snow-covered surfaces. Each is compiled in only while its World knob is on.
