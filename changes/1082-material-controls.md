Four landing concept lab scenes gain material controls that change their simulations rather than only their look, each leaving its default unchanged. The Chladni plate's grain size runs from fine sand, which creeps into thin, crisp lines, to grit, which is thrown quickly into broad, rough, steeper ridges with loose grains around them. The lava lamp's wax body runs from silky wax that rises in smaller blobs, slips through itself and trails long tails, to chunky wax that moves in fewer, slower, knobbly lumps. Incense has an optional ceiling vent that draws the layered smoke away along the ceiling without bending the fresh thread, as turning up the draft would. The ripple tank's edge absorption runs from beaches that swallow every wave to bare walls whose reflections fill the tank with standing waves, independently of open-water damping.
