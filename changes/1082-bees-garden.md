The landing concept's bees scene has a Garden group. Flowers sets how thickly the drifts bloom: 1 is the garden as it was, lower thins them down to bare grass at 0, and higher makes them denser and wider, with more poppies, daisies and foxgloves near the lens; near 3 the drifts run down the slope to your feet. A lantern can hang from an iron crook among the hives. By day it is unlit; after dusk it glows warm and lights the hives, the path, the nearby flowers and the grass over a few metres, the hives throwing soft shadows away from it, and it has a halo in fog and rain. Fairy lights can run along the dry-stone wall and glow on its stones at night. Each of these is compiled in only while it is switched on, so the default afternoon draws exactly as before.
