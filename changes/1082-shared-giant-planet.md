The giant ringed planet in the landing concept's Campfire sky can now show what Orbit's planet does, from a new Giant planet section in the lab: moons on its ring plane that cross in front of it and slip behind it, casting their shadows, an aurora round its pole, city lights on its night side and its own day sweeping round it. All are off by default, leaving the planet as it was. Both scenes now draw the same planet, and Orbit's planet keeps its look when made small: fine belts, ring grooves and boulders fade instead of shimmering, and its city lights merge into fewer, larger towns.
