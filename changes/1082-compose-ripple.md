The landing concept's ripple tank is now a preset of the `overhead` composer (ADR 0062 Phase 4), in two layers: the tank (`tank`, the bed: the screen or table, the tray and the lamp's colours) and its waves (`tankWater`, the water: the GPU wave equation and the experiments set up in it, which now own the `focus` knob), with the pond's koi offered as a third. It draws the same pixels as before at its defaults and in the "walled tank" preset; every knob keeps its key, and its "now" buttons are numbered by the composer and read by the sim from its knobs.
