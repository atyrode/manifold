In the landing concept, the Murmuration's emergent birds now fear the falcon along the line of sight through it. The birds it is seen passing through scatter across that line, so the hole opens on screen, where before a sphere held only about 60% of them. Fireflies has a new `unison` knob, off by default: each firefly's own pace adapts toward the beat it sees (Ermentrout's frequency adaptation), so the bank stops pulsing as a wave and flashes as one, within tens of milliseconds, as mangrove fireflies do. The buzzard reads apart from the rooks in a still frame: it is drawn at least 10 cells wide, and when soaring above the eye it shows its pale underwing with dark tips. The Street crossing's tile grid now covers the widest frames, with an assert that every agent slot fits the tiles' byte ids. Each tile lists up to 16 bodies, and the street holds up to 54 vehicles. A walker the watchdog gives up on in view now walks to the nearest way out instead of vanishing. Skyward works out its sky once per pixel instead of at three call sites, for the same picture and a smaller program.
