The landing concept lab has a tide pool prototype: a rock pool seen from above, where a simulated swell breaks over the reefs and, in its bigger sets, pours through a notch in the pool's rim, and the pool keeps what comes over. Kelp and surfgrass stream with the water, anemones close at a surge, a touch or low water, and the caustics on the floor come from the water's own surface. The tide, swell, sets, life, rock and light are all lab controls, with time of day, rain, bioluminescence and a pebble M. The water, still-water filling and caustics are shared building blocks other scenes can use.
