The landing concept's lab is now one floating inspector over the scene instead of a sidebar, with a transport bar for motion, pause, transitions, every "now", the A/B comparison, the tour, undo, shuffle, reset and links. The inspector has three views, picked by the icons in its header: Scenes, every scene's thumbnail shelved by family (sky and space, water, land, matter and patterns, rooms and life) with a search; Presets, this scene's setups, the ones saved in this browser with export and import, and the baseline by family; and Controls, the scene's groups under icon headings with a control search. A rail beside each view jumps between its families, preset groups or control groups, and the scene's name in the header or the transport bar opens the scene picker. The inspector and the bar drag, dock to the edges, resize, fold to an icon and keep their place in this browser; on a narrow screen they become a bottom sheet with the same three views. Knobs get purpose-built controls where their metadata asks for one: a stacked mix bar for the countryside's field types, a sky dial for the time of day, the day cycle and the moon's phase, vector pads for the wind and currents, position pads for the pond's sun and stone, and icon chips for switches. Every knob with an event now has its own lightning "now", the stone garden's leaf fall included. Links, presets and undo are unchanged.
