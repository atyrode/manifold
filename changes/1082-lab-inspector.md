The landing concept's lab is now a workspace of floating panels over the scene instead of a sidebar: a scene browser with thumbnails shelved by family (sky and space, water, land, matter and patterns, rooms and life) and a search, an inspector with icon headings, a rail to jump between groups and a control search, and a transport bar for motion, pause, transitions, every "now", the A/B comparison, the tour, undo, shuffle, reset and links. Panels drag, dock to the edges, fold to an icon and keep their place in this browser; on a narrow screen they become a bottom sheet with tabs. Knobs get purpose-built controls where their metadata asks for one: a stacked mix bar for the countryside's field types, a sky dial for the time of day, the day cycle and the moon's phase, vector pads for the wind and currents, position pads for the pond's sun and stone, and icon chips for switches. Every knob with an event now has its own lightning "now", the stone garden's leaf fall included. Links, presets and undo are unchanged.
