A plugin's server half can now open a door of a plugin its manifest declared as a dependency, with `ctx.actions.call({ plugin, action, input })`, in realm or hardened. The callee runs under the principal of the request the caller is serving, so its capability checks grade whoever asked rather than the calling plugin and nobody gains authority by being called; the calling plugin is recorded as the origin on the callee's ledger row, beside the row of the dispatch it was serving, and those two keys are the ledger's own — a request cannot forge them. Only a declared `required` or `optional` edge is admitted — anything else is refused `undeclared_dependency` naming both plugins, an optional dependency that is absent or switched off is `dependency_unavailable` with the caller still serving — and the callee door's declared capabilities must also lie inside the calling plugin's own ceiling, so a plugin never does through a dependency what its install grant withheld from it (`caller_ceiling`, naming the caller, the door and the capability). The callee's own answers arrive as `unknown_action`, `capability` or `refused` carrying its sentence, and a callee that breaks is reported as a failure of the edge rather than as its own error text. One trace may carry eight plugin frames and may not revisit a plugin, refused as `dispatch_depth` and `dispatch_cycle`. The closed class list, the member and the depth bound are published at `GET /api/protocol` under `pluginContract.actionCall`.
