The landing concept's fluid scenes (incense smoke, soap film and pearlescent fluid) now keep their flow incompressible at the scale of the whole picture, not only in small eddies. Smoke, film and satin no longer pile up into bright blobs where the flow converges or thin out where it spreads, and a turbulent incense plume tears into cleaner, sharper curls instead of swelling into a milky cloud. The scenes keep their look at their default settings, and the simulation costs about what it did.
