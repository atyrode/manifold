The landing concept lab gains a shared world: one atmosphere stack (sky with sun, moon phase, stars, Milky Way and shooting stars; clouds and their shadows; aurora; birds; rain, snow, fog and lightning; wind) that a scene draws through a description of its camera and the distance to what it shows, so birds pass behind nearer land and rain and snow fall in front of it, with the same knobs in every adopting scene and each part built into a scene's shader only while its knob is on. Meadow, shore and the dunes are the first scenes on it and each gains the features it lacked (the shore its moon phase, Milky Way, aurora, gulls and snow, reflected in the sea; the dunes clouds, birds, rain, snow and lightning; the meadow a Milky Way, snow, fog, lightning and cloud shadows), and they now open on a livelier default world with fair-weather clouds, birds by day and the Milky Way on dark nights.
