The landing concept's rooms now share one shell and take their light from the World. The back wall, a window's frame and glass and the ledge (a sill, a sideboard, a stand or a low table) are drawn in metres, and the sill, the lava lamp's room and the lab's empty room stand on it. Each room faces a way, so the sun's and the moon's patches move through its windows with the hour and the date, greyed by overcast, dappled by rain, flashed by lightning and tinted by the aurora; the bonsai's sun direction, the incense's angle and tint and the lava lamp's moon knob are gone, the lava lamp and the incense keep their midnight and the frost its midwinter night. Lamps, candles, neon tubes, fires and windows are light elements carried in one `elements=` link key; a table lamp, a pillar candle, a neon tube or a fire bowl in frame draws its body, and a room holds at most 8 lights, 2 of them windows. The bonsai has one snow, the World's, which its rain becomes in the tree's winter.
