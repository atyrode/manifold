The landing concept's eight rooms (sill, lava lamp, bonsai, incense, frost, late bar, night train and mobile) are now variants of one interior baseline, each stored as only its differences from it: the layers it adds and removes, its knob values, its camera at rest, its room and its grade. The baseline is an empty room at dusk at the sill's rest pose, with the window onto the city over a bare sill. The lab shows it as a new scene, Room, at `?scene=room`; it stays out of the tour and out of stepping with `[` and `]`. A test refuses a variant difference that changes nothing. Every room, its presets and its links draw as before.
