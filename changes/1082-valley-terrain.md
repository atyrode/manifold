The landing concept lab has a valley prototype built on a new shared terrain toolbox: one map of a river valley carved by running water, seen from a hill at its corner, with the river lying level in its bed and widening downstream, a village, hamlets, farms and a castle where people would build, roads between them over one stone bridge, hedged fields along the contours, woods on the steep ground and trees along the hedges, under the shared sky, clouds and weather. Landscape, relief, erosion, woodland, river size, settlements and roads are lab controls; each landscape change rebuilds the valley over a few frames while the last one stays on screen.
