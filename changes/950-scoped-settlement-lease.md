Operators can give selected plugins a finite settled-job callback allowance through `MANIFOLD_JOB_SETTLED_TIMEOUTS`, without extending transition hooks or ordinary actions. The original job credential and callback lease fence new data, job and sibling-action authority after completion, expiry, revocation or teardown; queued isolate calls cannot outlive their requests. Installed in-realm and hardened-plugin smoke exercises retained a 15-second callback under a 30-second allowance while default and selected finite deadlines still refused late writes, and regressions cover late return, queued work and deferred sibling admission.
