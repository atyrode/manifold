The landing concept's tide pool and river delta now share one shallow-water simulation that carries breaking waves at their true speed and height: a wave front that used to lag by up to a third and stand too high behind a hump now matches the exact solution within a percent, still water stays still over any rock, and no water is made or lost. The tide pool's swell and white water and the delta's river are retuned to keep their look: the surf washes as far up the rock and leaves as much foam as before, with each breaker now running in on time.
