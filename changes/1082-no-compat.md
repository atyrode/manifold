The landing concept lab now reads links, presets saved in the browser, both slots of an A/B comparison and tours in its current settings only. A setup made on an earlier build no longer has renamed settings or retired scenes carried over (the countryside's old seasons, the night skies' single giant planet, the aurora fjord scene, the bees' former cloud cover and colony sizes of 32 and 64): it opens as the current controls read it, and still replays exactly on the build it was made with from its `@<commit>` link, as each preset's arrow in the Presets view does. A link naming a scene the lab does not have still opens the default scene and says so.
