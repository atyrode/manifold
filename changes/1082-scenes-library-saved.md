The landing concept's lab has one Scenes view instead of separate Scenes and Presets views, and keeps what ships apart from what you saved. A switch over its search picks the **Library** or **Saved**. The Library holds what ships: the engine's bare canvases and examples, then every scene shelved by baseline, each card counting its presets; right under the scene on screen stand its presets and, for a variant, Compare with its baseline. Saved is everything saved in this browser in one list, newest first, setups and canvas documents side by side with a picture each: save what is on screen under a name, open, delete, download a document, and export or import the whole list as one file. An import never overwrites what is saved: a document under a name already taken gets the next number. The canvas's Document section keeps its name, Save, New and Export; its list of saved documents and its Import moved to Saved.
