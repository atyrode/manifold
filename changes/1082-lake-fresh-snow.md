The landing concept's frozen lake has a "fresh snow" control for how recently snow fell. At its default the patches stay old, wind-packed snow with low ridges and crisp edges, as before; raising it turns more of their surface to fresh powder, smoother and brighter, white even where it lies thin, its edges feathering into a dusting over the black ice and more crystals glinting in it, while about the same share of the ice stays covered.
