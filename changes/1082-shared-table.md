The landing concept's harmonograph is a shared table: everyone present draws on its sheet in the pen's own ink and nib. A tap lets a small pendulum plotter go at that point, its frequencies detuned by a hash of whose hand it was and when it pressed; it draws its little dying figure and stops. A drag draws freehand. While hands work the sheet its ink slowly fades and a finished figure is swung again on the same sheet instead of sliding it away, and the cast's agents go to its open margins. With no agents and no visitor input it draws exactly as before. The other scenes' hands follow: bamboo, the bonsai, the bar, the Chladni plate, dew, the garden's gravel, the lake's ice, the lantern river, marbling, the mobile, the street, the petri dish, the slime mould, the delta and the tide pool now answer every principal's press, drag or passing (taps of others where one tool can be held), declare the places where a press does something for the agents to go to, and the scenes that follow one hand (fractal, hyperbolic, prism, ferrofluid, lava lamp) say why they keep the visitor's.
