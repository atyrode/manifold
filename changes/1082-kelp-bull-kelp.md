The landing concept kelp forest can now grow bull kelp as well as giant kelp: a new "kelp" choice switches to shorter bare stipes, each held up by a single gas bulb with a fan of long strap blades streaming from it, swinging through the surge under an open surface, or mixes the two. Giant kelp stays the default and looks exactly as before, and a mixed forest keeps the same giant plants where they stood.
