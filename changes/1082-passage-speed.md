Scene transitions in the landing concept lab now read as one continuous movement: nearly every scene's exit accelerates away and is still moving when it hands over, and its entrance arrives already moving and then settles, instead of both coming to rest at the seam. The incoming scene is also readied and run off screen before the leaving one starts its exit, so a scene whose world builds over several frames or on a worker (the countryside's land, the pond's plants) enters with it in place rather than popping it in, and a lab check measures every scene's speed across the seam.
