The landing concept's marbling scene has a practice control that separates how the ink is laid from the inks and the pattern: ebru lays a ground colour and flicks stones onto it, suminagashi touches ink and clear water in turn into rings that, left uncombed, are turned by breath and a fan. Any palette can now be worked either way, such as risograph inks in suminagashi rings or sumi and indigo in ebru stones; the default follows each palette's own practice, so existing setups look as before.
