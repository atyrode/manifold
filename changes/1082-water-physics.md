The landing concept's water is more physical. The shared shallow-water solver gains a second-order mode (MUSCL–Hancock, still well-balanced and never running dry), and the tide pool runs on it, so its swell arrives as tall at every wave length; its swell, friction and foam are retuned so the default sea looks as before. The moon's, sun's and lamps' glitter paths on the shore, island, swell, frozen lake, fjord and koi pond follow one physically based glint: low lights lay long, blazing paths and high ones short, faint ones, rougher water widens and dims them, and the island's lantern and the lake's cabin window fade with distance. The swell's crests are as sharp as the waves it draws, the koi pond's ripple crests run through their rings as real ripples do, and the island's near ripples follow the wind.
