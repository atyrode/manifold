In the landing concept's countryside, fog lies in the valley again instead of on the hills. It used to be thickest on high ground and barely touch the river. Now a bank follows the river down the valley, with slopes, ridges, trees and roofs rising out of it. Its top drifts in slow patches, and the far ridges and horizon sit in the same fog without a seam. It is thickest at dawn, burns off through the morning and returns toward dusk, and lasts longer in a cold season. It glows toward a low sun and dims at night, with soft halos round the lit windows. With fog off the scene is unchanged.
