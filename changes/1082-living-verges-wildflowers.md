In the landing concept lab, the Countryside scene has a new verges control (off by default): clumps of cow parsley, red campion and tussock grass at the foot of the hedges nearest the view, swaying in the wind and bowing as its gusts roll across the fields, following the farming season, carrying the snow, and keeping off the roads, the water, the yards, the woods and the grazed pastures. The Meadow scene has a new wildflowers control (off by default): clumps of ox-eye daisies, buttercups, knapweed and field scabious standing out of the grass, bowed by each gust and back at rest in still air. Both are compiled into their scene only while switched on, so the default pictures are unchanged.
