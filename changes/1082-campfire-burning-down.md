The landing concept's Campfire has an optional burning-down fire: its logs catch and flare, char black with glowing cracks, thin where the flames are hottest and slump into the coals with a shower of sparks, then crumble into a bed of embers that greys to ash, over a chosen number of minutes per log. Someone tends the fire, laying a fresh log on (smoking until it catches) whenever one has burnt away or the flames run low, or you can let it burn out or lay one on yourself with "now". It is off by default, leaving the steady fire exactly as it was.
