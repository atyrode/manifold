The landing concept's bamboo grove gains a knock control for its water clock. At its default the tube still clacks hard on bare stone, bounces, throws drops from its mouth and can startle a leaf from the canopy; turned down, a pad of moss on the striking stone takes up the end of the swing, so the tube eases down with a soft knock on the same beat, without a bounce or drops, barely rings the basin and startles no leaves.
