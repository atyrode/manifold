The landing concept lab's pendulum wave has a physics choice. Ideal, the default, keeps the exact small-angle swing that brings the row back into line on the dot. Real swings each pendulum as a real one swings: a board draws every bob back as far as the longest one swings, so the shorter ones swing wider, and a wide swing takes a little longer than a small one, so the row slips off its schedule and only roughly lines up again, the more so the wider the swing.
