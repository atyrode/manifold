The landing concept lab's bottom transport bar is gone: the lab is now one menu shown two ways. Expanded, it is the inspector panel as before, with a slim row of tools at its foot (play or pause, undo, redo, shuffle, and "⋯" for the motion, transitions, every "now", the A/B comparison, the tour, reset, the link, explanations, the shortcuts and the build's status). Compact, it is a bar along the bottom edge: the scene's chip opens the scene picker, a Presets chip the presets, and a chip for each control section (the world's, the scene's own and the global ones) opens that section's own controls in a popover; a chip lights while its section differs from its defaults and shows its section's switch, the chips go icon-only and then scroll when there is no room for their titles, and on a phone a popover opens as a full-width sheet. One button switches between the two, and the choice is remembered.
