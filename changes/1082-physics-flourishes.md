In the landing concept, three physics scenes now behave as the real thing does. The ferrofluid's spikes pop up only once the breathing field passes the threshold, and they stay up a little below it before dropping, so the crown rises late and lingers; every breath now sinks it right back, however strong the magnet. At the finest end of the Chladni plate's grain size, some of the batch is powder that gathers in pale mounds where the plate swings hardest, while the sand still draws the lines. In Orbit, the moons circle at Kepler's rates, so the outer ones are no longer too slow, and a captured meteor speeds up as it spirals in. The harmonograph and the Chladni plate also send far less data to the GPU each frame, with no change to what they draw.
