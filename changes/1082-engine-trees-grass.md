The landing concept's engine grows trees and grass. A tree is an oak, a birch, a pine or a windswept pine grown from its seed, branch by branch, its crown clumps of leaves you can see the sky through up close; it leans and its limbs sway in the World's wind, harder as a gust reaches it, its shadow falls dappled, and its leaves come out in spring, turn in autumn and fall to bare twigs by winter. Grass is a patch of blades over the land, following its hills and hollows, with wildflowers through it; the wind bends it and every gust crosses it as a pale wave. The lab's Canvas shelf has a Grove to start from: trees of every kind in long grass on a gusty late afternoon.
