The landing concept's skies now run on one world clock. In the countryside and the garden the sun's height follows the date (higher and longer days by summer, lower by winter) while each scene's default picture stays as it was; a moon in any phase short of full stands where that phase puts it, a crescent low over the sunset, a half moon a quarter of the sky from the sun; with the day cycle on, the stars, constellations and Milky Way turn about the pole as the night passes; a full overcast now closes down to the horizon instead of leaving a band of clear sky, and far haze no longer paints the sun's or moon's disc into distant land near sunrise and sunset. The lab's sky dial, "See it at night" and the scenes' own simulations read the same sky.
