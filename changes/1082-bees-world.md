The landing concept's bees scene now stands in the shared world: its sky, weather and birds are the same as meadow's, shore's and dunes', so the valley gets rain that wets and darkens the garden, snow that settles on the lawn, hills, wall, hives and trees, fog into the hills, lightning, the Milky Way, swallows by day and a moonlit night whose light reaches the land. Its painted cumulus towers and their drifting shadows became an opt-in part of the shared world, on in bees and a cumulus switch away in every other outdoor scene that uses it; as rain or snow closes the sky they grey, flatten and sink into the cloud. The default afternoon is unchanged but for a few fair-weather clouds.
