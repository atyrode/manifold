The landing concept lab can now compare two complete setups, scene, knobs, look, motion and page, as slots A and B: tap `b` to flip between them or hold it to peek, each slot keeps its own undo, and the comparison travels with the page's link. It can also play a tour of chosen scenes through their own entrances and exits, each for a dwell of its own (30 seconds unless set) counted at the pace the scene grows, with play, pause and skip from the lab or the `t`, `[` and `]` keys; a tour link plays as it opens, except under reduced motion, where it waits for play.
