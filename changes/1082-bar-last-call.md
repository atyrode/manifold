The landing concept's Late bar can call last call from the time-of-night control's "now": the bartender rings a brass hand bell and the lamps blink twice, everyone at the bar looks up for a last sip and some order one for the road, and with nobody at the bar he comes over to you instead; with the bartender switched off, the lights are simply flicked from the back. It then stays closing time, with nobody new coming in, the room emptying, the lamps down and the OPEN sign dark, until the time of night is changed. Nothing changes unless it is called.
