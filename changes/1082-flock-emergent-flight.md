The landing concept's Murmuration scene has a new emergent flight switch. Off, as before, the starlings fly one choreographed sheet that stretches, banks, folds and parts on cue. On, every bird steers only by its nearest few neighbours, and the flock's own turns, dark knots, splits and startle waves come out of that; its amount sets how many neighbours each bird watches, about seven for real starlings. Switching either way hands the birds over where they are, so the flock never jumps.
