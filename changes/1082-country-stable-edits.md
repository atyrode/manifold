In the landing concept's countryside, switching a settlement, the roads or the tracks on or off no longer reshuffles fields far away: land use now changes only near the place switched and the ways it moves (one hamlet used to change a fifth of the map), the village keeps every cottage and its own street when the roads or tracks are off, and a given landscape's field pattern changes once with this fix.
