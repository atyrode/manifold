The landing concept's Dunes scene is now a preset of the outdoor composer, built from two layers that can be switched in its Layers section: the desert (the sand sea to the horizon under its dust, the heat shimmer and mirage, the sandstorm a hard wind raises from the fog) and the dune field (the live dunes, their ripples and blowing sand, the sandstone M, sand poured under the pointer). It draws exactly as before at its defaults, at night, in a sandstorm, in rain and snow and from overhead. The sandstone M's link key is now `dunesMark` instead of `mark`; the view from straight above keeps its `camera` key and stays the dunes' own until the overhead composer takes it.
