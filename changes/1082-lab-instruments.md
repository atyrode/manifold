The landing concept lab's controls work as instruments rather than lists: the time-of-day dial becomes an almanac whose outer ring sets the season (and the year's passing) in any scene that has one, or stands as a season wheel of its own; weather and sky groups offer one-tap recipes such as clear, drizzle, thunderstorm, snowfall, fog, golden hour or starry night that set only the knobs a scene has and light up while they hold; the stars, Milky Way, shooting stars and aurora share one small night sky to click and drag, the wind pad carries its gusts, and birds are chosen from drawn silhouettes and formation diagrams with a sky strip for how often they pass; a switch with an amount carries it on its chip (drag across it to set it), any value readout can be scrubbed, and every group has a roll that shuffles just that group; a lens shows only what differs from the scene's defaults, with marks on the rail and one reset for all of it; and the shared sky, weather, wind and birds of every scene sit together on a World shelf above the scene's own groups, with an opt-in keep switch that carries the time, season and weather into the next scene picked.
