The landing concept lab's controls work as instruments rather than lists: the time-of-day dial becomes an almanac whose outer ring sets the season (and the year's passing) in any scene that has one, or stands as a season wheel of its own; weather and sky groups offer one-tap recipes such as clear, drizzle, thunderstorm, snowfall, fog, golden hour or starry night that set only the knobs a scene has and light up while they hold; birds are chosen from drawn silhouettes and formation diagrams with a sky strip for how often they pass; every group has a roll that shuffles just that group; and the shared sky, weather, wind and birds of every scene sit together on a World shelf above the scene's own groups, with an opt-in keep switch that carries the time, season and weather into the next scene picked.
