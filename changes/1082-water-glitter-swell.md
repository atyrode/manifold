The landing concept's water scenes now share one model for the sun's and moon's glitter, which widens with the wind and toward the horizon instead of drawing a fixed spot: the island's sea, its lantern and the moon on it, the frozen lake's moon and cabin window paths, the koi pond's sun glint and the open swell all use it. The swell is rebuilt in full colour on the shared water: long swells with sharp crests that break white when tall, chop that breaks the moon's path into glints, and the shared night sky in its reflection, with every link keeping its settings. In the koi pond the ripples bend the floor by the water's depth, so deeper water shows them more and the look no longer changes with the pixel size.
