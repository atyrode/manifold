The landing concept countryside can now mix its original seasonal crops, wildflower meadows, lavender rows and sunflowers with independent relative-weight sliders. Zero excludes a type; all zero retains crops. Reproducible landscape seeds vary the hills, fields and village without reshuffling the valley when other controls change; weights and seeds work with URLs, resets and saved setups, including migration of older field-type recipes. Distant woodland now mixes broadleaf trees with adjustable evergreen pines, and its treetops no longer ripple the hillsides beneath them. Long choice labels wrap into readable rows in the lab.
