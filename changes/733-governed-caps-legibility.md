The permissions card now says which of three states each declared capability is in — granted, withheld by the installer, or governed and discharged per node by consent — instead of showing a governed capability struck through as one the installer refused. A plugin declaring nine capabilities and holding three in its grant is no longer described as an install that dropped six; and an installer whose explicit grant names a governed capability is told that no grant carries one, rather than being left to compare the notice against the manifest.
