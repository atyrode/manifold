The landing concept lab's soap film now bursts as one hole growing from the break. Its rim used to judge each point by that point's own thickness alone, so a thin patch beyond thicker bands, such as a plume rising from the wire or a spot blown with the pointer, could open on its own before the rim reached it; the rim now works out, as the film breaks, how long it takes to reach every point through the film in between, racing through thin film and slowing in the thick.
