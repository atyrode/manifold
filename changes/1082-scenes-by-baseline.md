The landing concept lab's Scenes view now shelves the scenes by baseline instead of by theme: a Room, a Land and a Patch shelf, each led by its baseline's own scene (tagged "baseline") and followed by its variants, then the standalone scenes on a shelf of their own. Setups saved in this browser appear on their scene's shelf as "custom" cards, under their name with the scene's thumbnail; a click loads the setup as the Presets view does, and saving, deleting or importing a setup in the Presets view shows on the Scenes view when it comes back on screen. The search also finds custom setups by name. `[`, `]` and the tour now step through the scenes in this shelf order, still skipping the three baselines.
