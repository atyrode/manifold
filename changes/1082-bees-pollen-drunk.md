The landing concept's bee garden is livelier and truer to an English summer. A few heavily loaded foragers now fly home pollen-drunk, fat and bouncy, in big curvy wobbly loops, bumping off the flowers, the hives' fronts and each other before they land; a new pollen-drunk control goes from today's sober colony to every bee reeling. Bumblebees climb each foxglove from its lowest bells to the top, also visit the poppies and daisies close by, and work only the flowers the flowers control grows, the lush garden's extra plants included. After dusk the grass now holds the steady green lights of glow-worms; the blinking fireflies stay as a control that is off by default. The insects are lit by the garden's light and seen through its fog and rain, so a swarm at night or in the mist no longer looks pasted on. Fireflies now shelter from rain and snow, butterflies fly off when fewer are asked for instead of vanishing, perched insects sit on their flower rather than beside or behind it, a hanging swarm stays on its branch when the window is resized, and the garden's opening run no longer stalls the first frame.
