The landing concept's Stone garden has an optional "drying with weather" control: freshly raked gravel then dries with the garden's conditions instead of over a fixed minute or two, its damp lingering in the groove bottoms while the crests pale first, faster with more weathering, in sun and wind, slower toward dusk and at night, and not at all in the rain, with an adjustable dampness. Off by default, leaving the garden as it was.
