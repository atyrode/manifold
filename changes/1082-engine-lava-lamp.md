The landing concept's engine can place a lava lamp anywhere: a true 3D rocket lamp of brushed metal and glass whose wax heats on the coil, swells, draws out a neck and tears free, rises, cools under the cap and sinks back, drawn as round blobs seen through the glowing liquid from any side and from above. It lights what stands round it in the liquid's colour, its base and cap throw a shadow, and its wax, liquid, bulb heat and size are its options. A new start card, Lava lamp, stands one on a timber plinth on the grass at night. Behind it, any kind of object can now run a simulation of its own per placed object, stepped on fixed steps so it plays the same at any frame rate, kept through moves and edits and dropped with the object.
