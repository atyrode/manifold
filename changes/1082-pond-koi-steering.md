In the landing concept pond, koi that are drawn to the mouse now notice the pointer moving over the water and swim over one after another, turning the way they face instead of sliding, and mill about it noses in without piling onto one another; once the pointer rests a while or leaves they drift back to their own loops. New "personal space" and "eagerness" controls tune how much room they keep and how far and how quickly they come. The pond looks exactly as before while the koi are not drawn to the mouse, and its koi are now built from a shared fish component other water scenes can reuse.
