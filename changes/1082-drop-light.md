The landing concept's falling drops now read in real colours. A drop is drawn as a lens: its head, a hanging bead and a splash's crown and droplets show the brightest light behind and over the eye (the sky, the sun or moon, and any lamp near it), light grey under a closed sky and white in sunlit drizzle, and a cell of the drop's body on either side of the head and its trail darken against the bright spray behind the waterfall's ferns. A wet mark darkens the stone and glints with the sky on a few of its cells as it dries. The waterfall's defaults draw the same as before.
