In the landing concept, dragging the Motion slider no longer makes things jump: the cloud deck, rain, snow and drops on glass, lightning, twinkling stars, the forest's light shafts and stream, the lava lamp's shelf plant, the bar's street and room, the ripple tank's still rings and the lake's aurora now only change pace. Rain keeps falling at real speed above motion 0.4 as before. A "now" taxi, song, mark glint, balloon or gust plays at the motion's pace without jumping either, and with Motion at zero the bar and the island no longer show a phantom song swell, taxi or balloon while nothing has been asked for.
