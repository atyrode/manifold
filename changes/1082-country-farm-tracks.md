In the landing concept lab's countryside, farmsteads near the track network now have short farm tracks running out to the nearest track once the tracks control brings it in, appearing with their track and fading in as plainly as it does. A farm track opens the hedges it crosses, stays on its own bank of the river and never moves a building; hedgerow trees rooted on it give way, pasture fences leave a gap for it and the herds keep off it.
