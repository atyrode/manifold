The landing concept's last three simulations now step on the one fixed clock too, so they play the same at 60 and 144 frames a second: the incense room (its smoke, dust, burner and doors), the pearl tank and the meadow. The smoke, the pearl's flakes and the meadow's grass, dandelion clocks and seeds are drawn between their last two steps, so they move smoothly however unevenly frames arrive. The meadow's opening pre-run now ends where its live steps begin, so no step runs twice; a reseeded meadow's pre-run takes only the frame's budget while the old meadow stays on screen, instead of stalling frames for up to 90 ms; a hand waved through the incense pushes the air as hard at any frame rate; and the incense and meadow warm-ups may use the frame's full time budget.
