The landing concept's Murmuration is now a preset of the `outdoor` composer (ADR 0062 Phase 2c), in two layers: the `roost` backdrop (the World's sky over a low skyline of rooftops, a reed bed on still water or the sea under a headland, only the one shown compiled in, the water mirroring the sky's dome and its cloud deck) and the `murmuration` life layer (the starlings' sheet or boids, far out in the sky so everything nearer hides them, with the falcon and the M), which the meadow, the bees' garden, the shore and the waterfall now offer too; it flies the same share of each rig's sky and pours down to roost when switched off. At its defaults the scene draws as before; the World's cloud, rain, snow, fog, aurora, shooting stars, moon phase and birds are new to it. The flock size is the murmuration's switch (`murmuration-amount`, was `birds`) and the M formation is `murmurationMark` (was `mark`).
