Star-joining in the landing concept: with the lab's agents in a scene and its constellations out, the agents now take turns leaving the page after nightfall to join a figure's stars, its lines growing star by star behind a cursor that moves among the stars and turns with them. The lines stand until dawn fades them with the stars, and the next night the figures are joined afresh, starting one figure further on. It works in every scene with the shared constellations. A new `star-joining` knob beside them sets how many of the six figures are joined a night; it is on by default and does nothing without agents.
