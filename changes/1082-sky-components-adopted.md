More landing concept scenes now share the sky components first built for Frost. Autumn, Bees, Dunes, Waterfall, Meadow and Sill get a stars control, the moon's phase, an aurora and shooting stars with a "now" button. Lantern river gets stars, an aurora and shooting stars, all mirrored in the river except the stars. Skyward gets the moon's phase and shooting stars. The frozen lake gets shooting stars in the sky and in its ice, Northern lights gets them over its fjord and mirrored in the water, and Swell gets them over the sea, glinting in the waves below. Bamboo, Forest and the city crossing get a stars control and the moon's phase, and the stone garden gets the moon's phase, which sets its light at night. Each new control starts at the picture the scene already drew, or off. A switched-off moon now sets below the horizon on its own bearing, so scenes lit by the moon never get a direction of zero.
