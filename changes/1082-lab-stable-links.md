---
section: Changed
issue: 1082
---

Links to the landing concept lab now name the version of their settings where they need it, so a link, a preset saved in the browser or either slot of an A/B comparison made on an older build opens with the settings it was made with, and a scene that is later renamed or merged into another opens its successor in links, presets and tours alike. A link naming a scene the lab does not have opens the default scene, as before, but the lab now says so in one line over the scene instead of switching silently. A link with a fractional number of tones, such as `levels=3.5`, now opens at the nearest whole number of tones.
