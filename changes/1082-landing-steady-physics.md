The landing concept's swinging things now move the same on every display: kelp, the dewy web, the hanging mobile, swaying plants, bamboo and the fireflies' lantern step at a fixed rate and are drawn smoothly between steps, so a 144 Hz screen shows the motion a 60 Hz one does. Threads and chains keep their swing instead of quietly losing it to the solver, with their stiffness and damping retuned so kelp, dew and the mobile look and feel as before, and resizing the window no longer scatters the dew: the drops stay where they hang on the web.
