The landing concept's outdoor scenes have a baseline: the plainest land, an eye standing in an open field with the chalk downs beyond under a fair afternoon sky. The lab shows it as a new scene, Land, at `?scene=land`; it stays out of the tour and out of stepping with `[` and `]`. The meadow is the first outdoor scene stored as only its differences from it: the grass and the dandelions, its eye in the grass, its bare earth, its golden hour, its shooting stars and its birds' flyway. A variant can now also set the World it opens with, its shooting stars, its flyway, its floor and its compile-time features. The plain floor, which drew flat and near-black wherever no ground layer covered it, is now lit by the open sky and mottled by its kind; no scene's default or committed preset shows it. The Presets view shelves the outdoor scenes under Land: the meadow as its variant, the others after it until they are ported. The meadow, its presets and its links draw as before.
