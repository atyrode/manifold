The landing concept's northern-lights fjord is now a place of the frozen lake rather than a scene of its own. The lake's new place control switches between the frozen lake and the fjord, with its open water, mountains, red cabin and birches, its fjord ice and its wind ripples. The fjord's slow drapes, with their activity, corona and green-only colour, are a choice in the lake's aurora section, and they now brighten and quicken in the lake's substorms. An "Aurora fjord" recipe sets up the old view in one tap. Old aurora links and presets open on the lake's fjord, keeping their settings. The frozen lake's own default view is unchanged.
