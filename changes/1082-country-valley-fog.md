In the landing concept's countryside, fog lies in the valley again: it gathers along the river and the low ground and thins with height, so the ridges, trees and roofs rise out of it and a crest stands sharp against the fog behind it, rather than hazing the hills while the valley floor stayed clear. It is thickest at dawn, burns off through the morning and gathers again toward evening, glows toward a low sun, and dims and haloes the lit windows inside it at night; with fog off the countryside looks exactly as before.
