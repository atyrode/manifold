In the landing concept lab, moving by hand between two scenes of one baseline (the room and its variants such as the sill and the lava lamp, the land and the outdoor scenes, the patch and the scenes seen from above), whether from the Scenes view, with `[` and `]` or with Next scene, now keeps the World and blends the two pictures. The next scene opens under the hour, weather, sky, planets and clocks of the one you left, whatever the World's keep switch says, and a running day cycle carries on where it stood. Neither scene plays its exit or entrance: once the next scene is ready, it dissolves in over the one you left, both still moving, in an even dithered crossfade of 0.8 s. Moving across baselines, to or from a standalone scene, or through a tour or a link still plays the passage. With passages off, or under reduced motion, the change is instant. One undo still goes back. The keep switch and an A/B flip now also carry a running day cycle on rather than starting it over.
