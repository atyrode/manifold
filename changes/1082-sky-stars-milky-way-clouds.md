Above the clouds, Murmuration and the shore get the shared stars control, starting at the stars they already drew; on the shore it sets the glints in the wet sand too. The Milky Way over the campfire and over the frozen lake is now one shared band with a brightness control, from faint to twice as bright, or off. Clouds became one shared component in tiers: the night train's calm deck, the deck every sky draws, its shadows, and the sea of cloud tops below the plane window. The autumn park can now let the shadows of its clouds sweep across the lawn, off by default. Every scene's default picture is unchanged.
