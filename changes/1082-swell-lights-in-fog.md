In the landing concept lab's Swell scene, the boats' lanterns and the lighthouse's lamp now glow round and soft through fog, rain and snow instead of being cut into rectangles: the air between you and each lamp dims its bright point, and the light it takes away spreads into a wider, softer aura the thicker the air, so a near lantern stays sharp while a far one in thick fog becomes a broad glow. The lighthouse's beams also light the air smoothly at every heading, so a beam sweeping toward you no longer splits the picture along a hard diagonal edge; as it passes over you the lamp flares and the air around you lights up.
