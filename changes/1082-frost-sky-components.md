The landing concept Frost scene's window sky now has controls for its stars, the moon's phase, an aurora and shooting stars, built as shared sky components that other outdoor scenes can adopt. A thinner moon dims the snow, the clouds and the room. The aurora tints the snow green while the sky is open. Shooting stars cross the sky over the roofs now and then, or at once with "now". The snow cloud hides them all, and thick frost softens them. The default picture is unchanged: full moon, aurora and shooting stars off. Every setting works with URLs, reset and saved setups.
