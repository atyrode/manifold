The landing concept's shan shui handscroll has a season control that lays light colour washes over the ink after Guo Xi's four mountains: spring blossom pink at the foot and young green up the slopes, summer's deep green, autumn's ochre turning russet with maple leaves among the moss dots, and a winter snow scene whose grey-washed sky and water leave the snow on the peaks and ledges as bare paper. Over the blue-green palette a season lies as a glaze; "none", the default, keeps the scroll as it was.
