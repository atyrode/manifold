The landing concept's night train has a line control that sets the country and what passes the window together. Country is the line as it always ran. Rural is a slower branch line across low, open farmland, with short halts where the train all but stops (most with a farm road crossing at the platform's end), level crossings everywhere, little girder bridges over streams and no tunnels. Mountain climbs slowly under high snowy peaks through forest and alpine pasture: long tunnels, often straight out of one onto a stone viaduct over a deep gorge and into the next, the hills rising over every tunnel, and few stations or crossings. The train also gains star density and moon phase controls from the shared sky, and optional birds (off by default) that cross the country by day: the line's own kinds unless one is picked.
