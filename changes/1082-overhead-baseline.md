The landing concept's scenes seen from above have a baseline: the plainest patch open to the sky, a dry pond floor of pebbles, moss and weeds under the World's afternoon. The lab shows it as a new scene, Patch, at `?scene=patch`; it stays out of the tour and out of stepping with `[` and `]`, and the pond's water, stone, border plants and koi can be switched on over it. The pond, the ripple tank, the tide pool, the sand table and the delta are now stored as only their differences from it, and the Presets view shelves them under it, each with "Compare with baseline". None of the five looks any different.
