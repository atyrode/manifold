The landing concept's synchronous fireflies are now a preset of the `outdoor` composer (ADR 0062 Phase 2b): three layers locked to its river, the `mangroves` backdrop (the bank's trees and the skyglow over them), the `creek` ground (the still river mirroring them) and the `fireflySwarm` life layer (the pulse-coupled fireflies, the lights nearer than the bank, the lantern and the M), each switched by a knob keyed by its id. The fireflies' glow and the lantern now light other layers as composer lights, and the fireflies hide behind anything nearer and dim in the World's fog and rain. Its own sky folds into the World (ADR 0062 D10): the stars and shooting stars are the World's, the moon off by default, so the stars differ from before; the bank, the river and the fireflies draw the same pixels at its defaults and at midnight. The river now takes the World's rain, fog, snow, cloud and lightning, and the bank its daylight. Keys: the breeze is the composer's gusts (`gusts`, `gustRate`; `unrest` is gone), the forest's seed is `seed`, `mist` is `creekMist`, `period`, `drift`, `lantern` and `mark` are `fireflySwarmPeriod`, `fireflySwarmDrift`, `fireflySwarmLantern` and `fireflySwarmMark`, and the sky's `stars` and `meteors` are the World's. The World now works out before a program runs what its setting settles: the shower's and the storm's own events only while their knobs are on (their "now" buttons always), the moon's phase only with a moon, and the sun's light only at hours it can reach the sky (`WORLD_METEOR_RATE`, `WORLD_LIGHTNING_RATE`, `WORLD_MOON`, `WORLD_DAYLIGHT`); `worldSkyOver` draws the World's sky over a dome of a scene's own, so the mangroves' whole night never works out the World's dome; a snowless plain floor (water) skips its snow. Every other scene draws exactly as before. Fireflies' camera now travels further at its passage's seams, so they keep their speed now that the World's stars no longer carry it.
