The landing concept lab's Countryside is rebuilt on a shared terrain map: hills carved by running water, a river lying level in the bed it cuts and widening downstream, a village, hamlets, farms and a modelled castle where people would build, and roads between them over one stone bridge, seen from a hill at the valley's corner under the shared sky, clouds and weather. The land shape (valley, coast, plateau or rolling hills), relief, erosion and a landscape seed reshape it.

Every parcel of land now has one use, chosen by how well its ground suits each use: crops, woodland, pasture with its herd of cattle or sheep, wildflowers, lavender or sunflowers. A single land-use mix sets their shares, each part switched on or off and then tuned, so the land runs from no woodland to entirely forest; raising one share converts only the parcels best suited to it. The village, hamlets, farms, castle, roads, farm tracks and river are switches with amounts in the same way, and a growing village adds cottages along its roads without moving any.

The farming season (summer, harvest with round bales, autumn woods on the turn with evergreen pines), village lights, chimney smoke, verges at the hedges' foot, the dusk starling murmuration, a chalk M cut into a hillside facing the view, snow trodden by walkers and carts, and wind ripples on the river are all available on the new map. The separate Valley prototype is folded into the Countryside; earlier Countryside setups still replay exactly at the build their presets were tuned on.
