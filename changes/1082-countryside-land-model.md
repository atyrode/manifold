The landing concept lab's Countryside is rebuilt on a shared terrain map: hills carved by running water, a river lying level in the bed it cuts, a village, hamlets, farms and a modelled castle where people would build, and roads between them over one stone bridge, reshaped by land shape, relief, erosion and a landscape seed. Every parcel of land now has one use chosen by how well its ground suits it (crops, woodland, pasture with its herd, wildflowers, lavender or sunflowers), set by one land-use mix whose parts are switched on and then tuned, from no woodland to entirely forest; raising a share converts only the parcels best suited to it, and a growing village adds cottages along its roads without moving any. The farming season with harvest bales and autumn woods, village lights, chimney smoke, verges, the dusk murmuration, a chalk M, snow trodden by walkers and carts, and river wind ripples carry over to the new map. The separate Valley prototype is folded in; earlier Countryside setups still replay exactly at the build their presets were tuned on.
