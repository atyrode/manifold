The landing concept lab has a new Mobile scene: a solar system hanging in a quiet room under a glowing Sun that is the room's lamp. Mercury to Neptune, with the Moon on the Earth's own small arm and optionally Ceres and Pluto, ride balanced wire arms stacked under the Sun at readable distances and sizes, Jupiter and Saturn the biggest, Saturn with tilted rings. The Sun lights each planet from where it hangs, so day and night sides change as the arms turn, and throws the mobile's shadows, rings included, across the wall. An optional motor, on by default, turns each arm at its planet's real relative period, sped up so the inner planets visibly go round, while the window's draft, its gusts and the room's air still swing and rock the arms and crowded arms still knock. Which planets, motor speed, clearance, draft, gusts, room air, a dark or daylit room, the Sun's brightness, soft shadows, window light and an M of glow-in-the-dark stars are adjustable, and a click blows on a planet. The mobile, now with balls, stacked arms and motors, is a reusable part other scenes can hang and light their own way.
