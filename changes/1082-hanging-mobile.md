The landing concept lab has a new Mobile scene: a Calder-style hanging mobile of balanced wire arms that swing, turn and knock against each other in a room's slow air, a window draft and passing gusts, throwing large shadows across the wall from a spotlight at night or soft daylight, with a second cooler light for coloured double shadows. Arms, plate shapes, paint, clearance, design, draft, gusts, light position and softness are adjustable, a click blows on a plate, and an optional Manifold M can be cut as one of the plates. The mobile and its wind are reusable parts other scenes can hang and light their own way.
