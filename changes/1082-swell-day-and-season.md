The landing concept's swell scene now reads as day by day and keeps a time of year. On the night page its ink still starts over a black point while the sky is dark, but as the day comes up it eases into a gentle curve, so the day's sky keeps its gradient, its cloud and the sun, and the water's own light turns the scene's blue under it, its glitter and foam left white; the moonlit night and the paper page by full daylight draw as before. A `season` knob (time of year, in the sky group, the autumn equinox by default, the date its sky is staged at) dates the World's sky and climate as the other dated scenes do: toward winter the sun stays low and comes up out of the sea in view, its glitter laid toward you, toward summer it climbs higher and rises out of view to the right, and the full moon swings the other way; in deep winter rain falls as snow.
