The landing concept's night skies compile in only the planets they hold: a scene's program now carries the surface and palettes of each kind its planets are, and their wide ring, moons, aurora or city lights only while one wears them, and draws a lone planet without the loop several take. Changing a planet's kind or palette, putting on one of those, or adding a second planet now builds another program, as turning a gated knob does. In software rendering Campfire, which opens with its giant, now draws in about the time it took before it was composed, rather than nearly three times that. Nothing on screen changes.
