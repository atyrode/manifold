In the landing concept lab's Fireflies scene, the mangroves are now living plants from the shared plant component: when a breeze crosses the river the crowns bow downwind tree after tree and swing back before standing still again, and the prop roots belong to the trees above them, each arching from under its crown down into the water. An optional shooting-stars control (off by default, with a "now" button) sends the odd meteor over the hills behind the trees.
