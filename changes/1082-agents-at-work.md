Agents at work in the landing concept: with the lab's agents in a scene, they now do calm chores that nature undoes. In the autumn park they rake the path's leaves into piles, a stroke at a time, and gusts scatter them. On the sand table they comb bands of parallel lines with a rake, and the ball's erase spirals groom them away. A drag on the sand rakes too; a press on the ball still leads it. On the shore they carry pebbles and balance them into cairns on the wet sand, a small rigid stacking sim with friction, and the bigger waves knock them down. Each chore is a knob in its scene, on by default, and does nothing without agents.
