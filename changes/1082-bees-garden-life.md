The landing concept's Bees scene has a livelier garden: the colony goes up to 200 bees, and a swarm (now, or now and then by itself) pours out of a hive, mills in a loose ball over the garden, hangs as a cluster in an apple tree for a while, then lifts off and leaves; bumblebees work the foxgloves and lavender, hoverflies hang dead still and dart, a dragonfly patrols the path, and fireflies rise from the grass after dusk. Everyone keeps the world's hours and weather: the bees go home at dusk and stay in through the night and in rain or snow, the butterflies roost, and by day there are no fireflies. The default afternoon looks as before, with a few more specks.
