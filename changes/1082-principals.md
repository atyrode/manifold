The landing concept's scenes see principals, not one pointer: the visitor is principal 0 and up to three agents of a scripted cast follow, each with a position, its button, a velocity and a kind. A new lab setting, Agents (Motion group, 0–3, default 0), brings the cast in: calm cursors that wander, go to what a scene says can be done, pause, press briefly and move on, deterministic from the scene's seed and its motion clock, drawn over the scene as small rings of its accent with a short wisp behind them. Ripples, koi, fireflies, the shore's plankton, frost, mist, smoke, the soap film, the pearl's eddies, the forest's spirits, kelp's fish and the dunes' poured sand answer every principal's hand; scenes that follow one hand keep the visitor's. Shaders read `uPrincipals` and `uPrincipalCount` in place of `uMouse`, and physarum's specks knob is now `specks` (it was `agents`).
