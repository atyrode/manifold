The landing concept lab's City crossing has an optional late delivery, off by default: now and then a white van crosses the junction and pulls in at the far kerb past the konbini, clear of the crossing, its hazards blinking while the driver wheels stacks of crates across the sidewalk to the shop's service door and back. Walkers step round the trolley, cyclists swing out round the parked van, and it pulls out when the lane behind is clear; its frequency and a "now" button sit with the traffic controls.
