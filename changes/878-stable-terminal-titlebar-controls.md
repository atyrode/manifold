Pressing a control in a crowded terminal titlebar, such as parking a mirrored terminal to the sidebar, no longer misses when your own presence badge appears in that titlebar mid-press. The titlebar reserves room for attendance before focus and lets directory and machine labels shrink without pushing controls onto a new row. Long labels now use character-level ellipsis, retaining the machine's color dot, rather than disappearing as a whole or clipping without a cue.
