Light elements now work outdoors in the landing concept: a link's `elements=` places lamps, candles, neon tubes or fires on the land, each lighting the lawn and whatever else takes local light around it and drawn where it stands, hidden by what stands nearer and veiled by fog and rain. A window or a shadowing element is refused outdoors, and scenes without elements draw exactly as before.
