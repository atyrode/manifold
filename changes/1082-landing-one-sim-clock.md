The landing concept's remaining simulations now step on one fixed clock and draw between their steps, so a scene plays the same however unevenly frames arrive: the ferrofluid pool and its spikes, the harmonograph's pen, the Chladni plate, the delta, the tide pool's fish, the slime mould, the prism and the sand table (whose springs now share one spring step), the bonsai, the mystic forest, the ripple tank, the fireflies, the campfire's fuel and the bees. Motion that used to stutter or ring faster on a slow or high-refresh display is smooth and matches the 60 Hz picture, and warm-ups may use the frame's full time budget.
