The landing concept's dewy web is now a preset of the `outdoor` composer (ADR 0062 Phase 2c), in two layers locked to its macro rig (eye 0.6 m, horizon 0.4, a field of 0.6 rad): the `bokeh` backdrop (the meadow, the hedge, the mist and the World's sky far out of focus, its `backdrop` choice) and the `web` dressing, the subject (the Verlet orb web and its drops on a row of the records atlas and two field slots, the spider, the M as `webMark`). It draws the same pixels as before at its defaults, in `view=source` and at sunrise; the composer has no focal blur, so each layer draws its own defocus and no other layer is offered the rig. New with the World: its rain and snow grey the sky behind the web and fall past it at the composed depth, its wetness darkens and its lying snow whitens the meadow and the stems, its fog fades the meadow and its lightning flashes on the dew; the stars, the cloud deck and the cumulus are not drawn in the blur. Keys: `webSeed` became the composer's `seed` and `mark` `webMark`; the switches `bokeh` and `web` and the World's knobs are new. The composer's grade takes inkOf's paper white and range (`Grade.ink.paper`).
