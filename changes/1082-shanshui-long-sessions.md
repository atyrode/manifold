The landing concept's shan shui handscroll now stays crisp and moves smoothly however long it is left unrolling. Over days its single painting coordinate had lost precision: the scroll began to advance in jumps, seal strokes broke up, and after weeks the landscape would have jumped about. The scroll is now painted in sections of 2048 screen heights (about six days at the default speed), each from its own origin, joined to the one before by a strip of mounting silk with the paintings dissolving into bare paper either side, as the sections of a long handscroll are joined; nothing changes before the first join.
