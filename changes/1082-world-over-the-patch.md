In the landing concept, the pond and the tide pool seen from above now take the World's hour and cloud. The sun, or the moon by night, lights them from where the hour puts it, so the koi's, the lily pads' and the light rays' shadows follow it; a passing cloud dims the whole pool, and the tide pool's water mirrors the sky and its clouds. Both scenes now have the World's time of day, day cycle, clouds and cloud shadows in the lab. The pond's sun position knobs are gone, since the hour now places its sun, and the tide pool's own sky knobs became the World's under the same names. Both open as before.
