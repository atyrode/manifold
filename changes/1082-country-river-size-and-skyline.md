In the landing concept's countryside, a new river size now changes only the river and the land beside it: the village, castle, hamlets and farms stay where they are and the fields elsewhere keep their crops (a small nudge used to move farms by up to two kilometres and change a quarter of the land). The far hills just under the skyline are now drawn where they lie instead of as nearer ground; walkers, carts, herds, buildings and the clumps at the hedges' foot stand on their ground at the edges of the frame instead of up to two-thirds of a cell off it; and where a pine wood meets a broadleaf one the trees now run on with no bare strip between them. The default landscape's settlements, roads and field pattern change once with this fix.
