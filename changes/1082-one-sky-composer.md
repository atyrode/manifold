The landing concept's autumn park, frost, window sill, bees, lantern river, waterfall, frozen lake and skyward scenes now draw their skies through the shared world sky, so a setting means the same thing in them as in the meadow, shore and dunes: rain and snow close their skies by the same overcast and dim the same light, and their stars, moon, aurora, shooting stars and cloud deck come from one composer in one order. Each keeps its look through its own view and tint (frost's town-lit snow cloud, the city's glow over the sill and skyward, the river's warm grey rain cloud), but their private moons and star fields are gone: the lantern river's young crescent and sparse stars give way to the world's stars and moon, which rises out of its narrow dusk view, and the frozen lake's moon stands where its phase puts it, high on the left for its default crescent and low over the mountains on the right as it nears full. Frost's default snowfall now leaves a few gaps in its snow cloud, and the lake's Milky Way is the world's band.
