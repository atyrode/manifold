The landing concept's autumn park, frost, window sill, waterfall, skyward and lantern river scenes now draw their skies through the shared world sky, as the meadow, shore, dunes and bees do: rain and snow close their skies by the same overcast and dim the same light, and their stars, moon, aurora, shooting stars and cloud deck come from one composer in one order. Each keeps its look through its own view and tint (frost's town-lit snow cloud, the city's glow over the sill and skyward, the waterfall's aurora lifted over its rim, the river's warm grey rain cloud). Their default pictures barely change; under rain or snow their skies and light turn as grey and dim as the other outdoor scenes'. The lantern river's private crescent and sparse stars give way to the world's moon, which rises out of its narrow dusk view, and the world's stars, now mirrored in the river too.
