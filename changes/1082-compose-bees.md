Agents at work in the landing concept's bees garden: a `gardening` chore layer on the apiary. While the lab's agents are about, they dig small beds in the lawn and sow them along a row; the seedlings grow into poppies, daisies, cosmos and wild flowers over a minute or two, the bees go to the new flowers, and weeds creep back so the agents return to weed. A bed left alone goes back to lawn, and when the last agent leaves its beds go with it. The layer is on by default in `bees` and costs nothing without agents: the beds' drawing is compiled in only with the agents' cursors. The outdoor composer gains two hooks for chores: layer sims declare points of interest for the cast, and a layer reaches its host's affordances.
