The landing concept's shared world now keeps one climate, one wind and one grey day, and its clocks never jump. A scene's season dates its sky (bonsai's private winter sun is gone), its rain turns to snow in the cold where the season sets the climate, and under rain or snow the sky its clouds leave open greys as the light does, in every world scene. The cloud deck drifts the way the rain slants and the cumulus drift. Turning the day cycle, the year cycle, the orbit's sky drift and day cycle or the pendulum wave's cycle length now changes their pace from that moment instead of jumping the sun, the stars, the date or the row. The moon can now wane, rising late and standing west of the sun in the morning sky; lesser moons light the land by the measured lunar curve, a garden's summer full moon now rises, the Milky Way no longer fades at a turning horizon, and the sky's fixed planets turn with the stars. Default pictures are unchanged except bonsai's light, kept close.
