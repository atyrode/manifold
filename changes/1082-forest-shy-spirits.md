The landing concept's mystic forest has a "shy spirits" control. At its default every kodama is curious and turns to look at the pointer, as before; raising it makes that share of them shy instead: when the pointer comes close they duck into the moss with their faces turned away and their glow drawn in, only their eyes peeking out, and creep back up a little while after it has gone.
