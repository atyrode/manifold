The landing concept's petri dish can hold its Turing pattern in one curated regime (fingerprints, coral, worms, mitosis, spots, mazes or holes) instead of wandering through them, each starting a fresh dish that turns into the chosen class within about twenty seconds rather than dying out. An optional Advanced section sets the feed and kill rates by hand for exploring the rest of the pattern map. The default dish still wanders as before.
