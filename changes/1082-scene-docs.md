The landing concept lab's scene docs now come from its scene registry: the scene guide opens with a table of every scene (its title, shelf, how it is built, how many controls and saved presets it has, and its one-line description), and its tables of the shared sky and weather controls each scene carries and of which scenes use each optional shader component are generated too, so they always match the lab; a check fails while any of them is out of date, or while a scene lacks its folder, shelf or thumbnail. The old scene backlog becomes the scenes' design briefs, no longer repeating those tables, and the tide pool is a full scene of the lab rather than a prototype.
