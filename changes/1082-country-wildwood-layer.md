In the landing concept's countryside, woods are now a layer of their own, the wildwood the fields were cleared from: they follow the land's own contours (lobes, copses, shaws along the streams) instead of filling whole fields, bite into the fields they reach, grow outward as the woodland share rises with one value sent to the GPU per drag, keep smooth edges up close, and no longer keep off the slopes under the viewpoint; the cattle share is now a share of the grazing land, so no other knob flips a pasture's herd; the chalk M is sited on the bare land against every planned way, so switching roads, tracks or settlements no longer moves it; and a ring of hills continues the land past the map's edge, so the land, not the sky, meets the eye wherever the view turns.
