Six landing concept scenes can now fly birds, from the same shared birds library as the Countryside: gulls over the night sea in Swell, swallows over the Meadow, swifts in the Waterfall's gorge, and skeins of geese over the Northern lights fjord, the Frozen lake and the Lantern river. In those last three the birds are mirrored in the water or the ice. Each scene's Birds control is off by default and picks how often a flight passes, which birds fly (rooks, swallows, gulls, geese, a buzzard, pigeons or swifts) and how they fly (as that kind usually does, alone, as a loose party, in a V or circling). Birds keep to each scene's hours and weather, and hills, cliffs, trees and nearer waves hide them. Skyward's pigeons and swifts now come from the same library, seen from below, and look exactly as before. Default pictures are unchanged, and every setting works with URLs, reset and saved setups.
