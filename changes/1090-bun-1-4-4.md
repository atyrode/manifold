section: Fixed
issue: 1090
---

The server image, the recovery image and the Nix packages now run on Bun 1.4.4, whose built-in root store no longer trusts certificates from the compromised eMudhra intermediate authorities (CVE-2026-91403). Under the previous Bun, anyone holding those keys could have impersonated any HTTPS endpoint the hub or its agents reached through Bun's built-in roots. Rebuild or redeploy from this release to pick up the new runtime; already-installed binaries and images keep the Bun they were built with. Source runs still need at least Bun 1.4.2, and Bun 1.4.4 is recommended.
