The landing concept's bonsai is now a preset of the `interior` composer (ADR 0062 Phase 3), in three layers kept apart as the room's shell and its subject: the veranda (the backdrop: the wall, plaster, shoji or a dark alcove, with the window's patch of sun creeping over it, the paper lamp's pool at dusk and the floorboards, its knobs the light and the hour), the rosewood stand (the ground) and the tree in its pot (a dressing: the tree, the moss, the stone and the figure, everything that falls and lies, the winter's snow). The tree's shadow on the wall is now the room's: a layer that stands in the room declares its silhouette (`casters`) and the composer gathers every on layer's into `roomCaster`, which the veranda's wall throws toward the sun and toward the lamp. The bonsai draws at its defaults exactly as before, and its keys are unchanged. The lava lamp is offered only to rooms whose eye stands 0.2 m or more over the ledge, so the bonsai's five "now" buttons and the lamp's two never pass the renderer's eight.
