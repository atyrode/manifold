The landing concept lab's night sky is one section of named controls under a small drawing of it: the moon picked by phase from drawn moons, the stars by density, and the Milky Way, aurora, shooting stars and two new shared sky components, constellations with their stick figures fading in by amount and a giant ringed planet, Campfire's, that sixteen scenes can now hang in their night sky with its size on its chip, a placement pad, a palette, its ring, moons, aurora, city lights and day cycle, each switched and set on its own, with a "See it at night" button while the scene is in daylight; both parts are compiled into a scene's shader only while switched on, so a sky without them renders as fast as before; the time dial's day cycle and the year's cycle are now one speed slider each, still at the left end, with readouts such as "a day in 4 min".
