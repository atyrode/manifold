The landing concept lab's night sky is one section of named controls under a small drawing of it: the moon picked by phase from drawn moons, the stars by density, and the Milky Way, aurora, shooting stars and two new shared sky components, constellations with their stick figures fading in by amount and up to five naked-eye planets, each switched and set on its own chip, with a "See it at night" button while the scene is in daylight; the time dial's day cycle and the year's cycle are now one speed slider each, still at the left end, with readouts such as "a day in 4 min".
