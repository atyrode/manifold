In the landing concept lab, the pond's border plants, the waterfall's near-bank ferns and the tide pool's kelp, surfgrass and anemones are now living plants from one shared plant component: each keeps its roots and structure, bends on its own with the wind, gusts or the water's flow, and can turn with the seasons and keep growing, new leaves unfolding as old ones yellow and drop. The pond keeps its arrangement and look, its stems and vines now swaying leaf by leaf instead of whole layers sliding together, and new seasons, growth and gusts controls (off by default) come to the pond, the waterfall and the tide pool. The tide pool can also host small rock pool fish that idle over the basin's floor and dart off from a touch or a surge.
