The landing concept's autumn park is now a preset of the `outdoor` composer (ADR 0062 Phase 2a): six layers, drawn through the park's own rig, in the order the park always painted them. `parkland` is the backdrop (the World's sky and the far crowns), `parkPath` the ground (the lawn, the gravel path and its pools), `lamp` and `maple` are dressings (the lamp post with its light and halo; the great tree with its bench and its leaves in the air), `litter` is a cover (the carpet of fallen leaves and the leaf M, now `litterMark`), and `rake` is the agents' chore over it. The composer brings the season (`season`, now the composer's), the seed (`parkSeed` became `seed`), the World's full set of knobs and one gust field: the canopy sways, showers fall and swirls lift off the path as the composer's gusts reach them, and rain and fog are the World's at the composed depth. At its defaults, at night and in rain the park draws as before but for a few cells the shared gust field and the World's rain move.
