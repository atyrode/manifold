The landing concept's window sill and lava lamp are now presets of the new `interior` composer (ADR 0062 Phase 3 pilot): a room seen by a level eye over a ledge, its back wall lit by the room's light (its lamp, its ambient, the window's daylight and the layers' own lights), and its layers painted back to front over it. The sill is five layers: the window onto the city (the backdrop, the World's sky and weather crossing its glass), the sill, the mug, books and plant, the cat and the curtain; the lava lamp's room is the papered wall, the sideboard and the lamp. Both draw at their defaults exactly as before. The lava lamp is drawn from the room's rig, 0.4 m tall wherever a room's ledge stands, so the sill offers it too: switched on, it stands on the sill beside the plant and throws its coloured light on the wall, the window frame, the sill, the mug and the cat. The sill's sky and weather knobs are now the World's, so it gains the Milky Way, cumulus towers and cloud shadows (off by default).
