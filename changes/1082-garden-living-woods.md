In the landing concept lab's Stone garden scene, the woods over the wall are now living trees from the shared plant component: in a breeze the crowns lean downwind and stir one by one, and a gust crosses them as a front, bowing the trees one after another before they swing back; in still air they stand exactly as before. A new border planting control (off by default, with an optional growth control) plants ferns and tufts of mondo grass in the gutter along the foot of the wall: they sway with the wind and gusts and follow the season choice.
