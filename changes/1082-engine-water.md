The landing concept's engine gains water: a pond, lake or pool set into the land anywhere, its outline round, oval, kidney-shaped or a pool's rounded rectangle, sized and deep as asked. Its surface ripples and chops with the wind blowing over it (cat's-paws drifting downwind, longer waves the wider the water), mirrors the sky and whatever stands round it (little looking down into it, nearly all at a glancing view), and shows its sandy, pebbly, silty or tiled bed through it, shifted by refraction, dimmed and tinted by depth, with the sun's caustics playing over the shallows. Rain rings it, foam gathers on the shore the wind blows toward, a wet bank runs round it, and with the cold (or always, if asked) ice creeps in from the shore, black and frosted, crazed with cracks. A new starter, Pond side, shows a kidney pond with a jetty and a lamp from its shore on an afternoon breeze.
