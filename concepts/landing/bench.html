GPU timing

Each scene at its defaults, drawn by the landing's renderer on this device on a canvas of the given cells, one per device pixel (960×540 is the budget a scene must hold at 60 fps on an integrated GPU; a larger grid is coarsened to it as on the landing, unless the page is opened with ?budget=off). The page times each scene's program compile and link, then its frames, drawn as fast as the display takes them: on the GPU when the browser offers EXT_disjoint_timer_query_webgl2, else from one drawn frame to the next. Nothing is sent anywhere: copy or download the report.
